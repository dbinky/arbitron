# Paired Reviews — Latest Model Profiles

Phase 2 runs only after every single-system box in `docs/reference/focus-areas.md`
is ticked. Each row joins two behaviours that meet all three conditions:

- they share persisted state, a wire contract, a flag or an ordering;
- different tasks or commits built them;
- each side passes in isolation while the combination is wrong.

One approval covers both directions. Trace the TRACE path, then show that BREAK is
impossible, either with a test that goes red or with a guard cited at `file:symbol`.
Area numbers refer to `focus-areas.md`; `G` numbers refer to `gaps-identified.md`.

**Budget:** 10 singles × 2 + 13 paired = 33 minimum passes ≤ 500 × 0.35 = 175.

## No seam

All 45 combinations of the 10 areas were considered: 13 are paired above and 32 are
recorded below. No seam is deferred for budget.

### Inside one behaviour

- Area 1 ↔ Area 4 (struck seeded row 3): `PinningResolver.Resolve` → factory → Claude/Codex verifier is an internal hop of the batch execution promise, not an independent probe hand-off; folded into Area 1 wiring check 6 and failure check 7.
- Area 1 ↔ Area 5 (struck seeded row 4): batch binding → pi/OpenCode/HTTP verifier is the same execution path; folded into Area 1 wiring check 7 and failure check 8.
- Area 6 ↔ Area 4 (struck seeded row 7): `HandleMessage`/`Execute` → `RequireExecutedModel` → Claude/Codex stream verifier is internal continuation wiring; folded into Area 6 wiring check 5 and failure check 6.
- Area 6 ↔ Area 5 (struck seeded row 8): HTTP stream identity validation before turn persistence is the continuation sink itself; folded into Area 6 wiring check 6 and failure check 7.

- Area 1 ↔ Area 2: both are sub-paths of the same trigger capture (`engine.go:captureModelPins`, `ModelPinSnapshot`). They were built by the same commit, `013bcda`, and are split only by route kind. The hand-off from Models to OpaqueRoutes is Area 2 wiring check 4.
- Area 1 ↔ Area 6: the shared `PinLookup`/`PinningResolver` contract was built by the same P1T3/P1T4 tasks. The batch and continuation entries stay separate areas. Release-versus-turn ordering is Area 6 failure check 5.
- Area 1 ↔ Area 9: a seeded pinned profile reaches the trigger through the same `ValidateSelection`/`ValidatePinRoutes` write contract that Area 7 failure check 6 traces. Seed's call to it is Area 9 wiring check 1.
- Area 2 ↔ Area 6: `RequiresModelEvidence` at `conversation.go:248` and `executor_interactive.go:136` was added by the same commit as the D8 enforcement (`ac2ad72`). The interactive D8 case is Area 2 wiring check 3 and Area 6 failure check 4.
- Area 2 ↔ Area 9: a seeded D8 pin uses the same `ValidatePinRoutes` contract as Area 7 wiring check 5, from the same commit `1fe44cb`. It is Area 9 wiring check 1.
- Area 4 ↔ Area 5: the lanes share only the router dispatch and `claudecli.RunSpec`, built in the same P3T4 slice. Each lane owns its own verifier; there is no independently built state hand-off.
- Area 7 ↔ Area 9: admin create and YAML seed run the same `ProfileService` prepare/revalidate/store flow (`writeTrusted`, `PreparedSelection`, `SeedBatch`) from the same P2T2 commit `1fe44cb`. They differ only by entry.
