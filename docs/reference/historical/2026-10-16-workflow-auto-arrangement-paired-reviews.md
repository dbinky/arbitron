# Paired Reviews — Workflow Auto-Arrangement Seams

Phase 2 of the loop: this table runs only after every single-system box in
`docs/reference/focus-areas.md` is ticked. Area numbers refer to that file.

Each row is one seam between two behaviours that were built by different tasks or
phases. The behaviours share state, a contract or an ordering, and the failure
would be silent: each side is green on its own. One approval covers **both
directions**. The reviewer follows TRACE and shows that BREAK cannot happen, either
with a test that goes red or with a cited guard at `file:symbol`.
