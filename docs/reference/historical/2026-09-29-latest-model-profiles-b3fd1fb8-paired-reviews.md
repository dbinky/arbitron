# Paired Reviews — Latest Model Profiles

Phase 2 runs only after every single-system review is approved. Each row joins
behaviours built by different tasks that share state, a wire contract or ordering
and can pass isolated checks while their combination is wrong. One approval covers
both directions; follow TRACE to the observable BREAK. Symbols in open B-gap paths
are planned contracts, not implemented evidence.

**Budget:** 9 singles × 2 + 16 paired = 34 minimum passes ≤ 500 × 0.35 = 175.

## No seam

All 36 combinations of the 9 areas were considered: 16 retained rows and 20
excluded pairs below. No seams are deferred for budget. Seed row numbers in this
section identify the pre-critique table only.

### Inside one behaviour or the same task (including struck seed rows)

- Seed seam 3, advancement ↔ execution gate: struck after both became Area 3; token validation and the connection-rotation race are internal wiring/failure checks of the same commit path.
- Seed seam 6, inventory ↔ probes: merged into row 7 after inventory became Area 3; revision keying and executable spelling share that evidence hand-off. Row 8 covers the other probe lanes.
- Seed seam 10, admin edit ↔ refresh loop: merged into row 6 after the loop became Area 3; the row retains both optimistic writes and bootstrap/coordinator ordering.
- Seed seam 11, refresh loop ↔ announcements: merged into row 14 after the loop became Area 3; publication/payload and retry-drain ordering now share one bidirectional row.
- **Area 1 ↔ Area 2:** P1T4 owns both consumers of the same pin decorator; their distinct entry points are single-area checks, not independent producer ownership.
- **Area 5 ↔ Area 6:** P3T4 owns the shared router and lane registration; no independently built wire contract between the lanes.

### Covered hand-offs (no additional independent seam)

- **Area 1 ↔ Area 7:** the explicit-version grammar is the same producer used by admin validation and trigger capture in row 11; policy-independent recognition remains an Area 7 failure check.
- **Area 2 ↔ Area 8:** the model change reaches the continuation through the same cache/pin boundary as row 2; Area 8 owns admin reload, and Area 2 verifies every turn consumes its pin.
- **Area 3 ↔ Area 4:** catalog URL and webhook reads belong to the ingestion and announcement consumers, traced in rows 10 and 16; there is no third settings hand-off to advancement.
- **Area 5 ↔ Area 8:** candidate initialization consumes the same evidence eligibility helper already traced in row 7; row 6 separately covers prospective-profile bootstrap and lock ordering.
- **Area 6 ↔ Area 8:** row 8 checks this lane's evidence contract, and row 6 checks its candidate consumer during initialization; no different probe contract for admin creation.
- **Area 5 ↔ Area 9:** persisted evidence enters the atomic commit through row 7, then the frozen payload crosses row 14; the sender never directly invokes a probe.
- **Area 6 ↔ Area 9:** row 8 covers evidence persistence, row 14 covers the frozen payload; no direct probe-to-sender protocol.
- **Area 7 ↔ Area 8:** membership and chronology reach admin validation through Area 3's shared CandidateService, covered by rows 9 and 6; the separate version grammar is row 11.
- **Area 8 ↔ Area 9:** frozen changes are produced by Area 3's commit and checked in row 14, including a later admin edit; delivery does not read the current profile.
