## Global constraints

The register stays `decided` until recorded evidence proves the installed version's controls, precedence and helper routing; then it becomes `verified`. Emitting a key the binary ignores is a defect: remove it (On-failure rule). An unroutable helper stops that backend's enablement and sets the item to `escalated`.

- Limits/efforts/settings come from LR-02 source notes; vendor/fixed-id/response spelling from LR-03. A completed short task establishes none of the maxima.
- Missing key/binary/required facts skips local experiments with a readiness-pending message. Release mode (`ARBITRON_LUNAROUTE_RELEASE_GATE=1`) fails missing prerequisites; no empty observation is success.
