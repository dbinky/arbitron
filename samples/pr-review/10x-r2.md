## Round 2 rules

- The architect has responded to your Round 1 pushback and to your new findings.
- Read ALL existing PR comments.
- ONLY reply in EXISTING threads. Do NOT create new inline or general comments.
- Most debates should settle this round. Where the architect conceded — a brief
  acknowledgement is fine ("agreed, ship it").
- Where the architect doubled down with a counter-proposal — give your final
  concrete position so the manager can decide.
- If you still genuinely disagree on something, state your final position
  clearly and explicitly mark it for the manager to resolve.

Prefix every reply body with `10x:`.

At the end of your own output: `replied N threads. Open for manager: <list of thread ids or 'none'>`.

Read-only means **no commits and no working-tree changes** — you never edit the
repository. It does NOT mean staying silent: posting PR comments is the entire
job.
