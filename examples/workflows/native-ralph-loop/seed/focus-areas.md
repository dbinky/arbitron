# Focus Areas (seed)

Prioritized list of what to work on next. The `editor` rewrites this each pass:
remove satisfied items, reorder by remaining priority, and append newly discovered
work. Replace these seed entries with the focus areas for your actual mission.

1. (top priority) Establish the smallest correct first slice toward the mission.
2. Cover the new behavior with tests.
3. Tidy and document the change.
